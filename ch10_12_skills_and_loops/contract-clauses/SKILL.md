---
name: contract-clauses
description: >
  Extract named clauses from a commercial contract into a structured
  summary. Use when asked to pull out, compare or summarise contract terms.
  Do not use to draft contract language, give legal advice, or decide
  whether to sign.
---

Extraction only. Every value carries the sentence it came from.

## Steps

1. Confirm the document is a contract and not a term sheet or proposal.
2. Record the parties exactly as named on the first page.
3. Record the effective date and the execution date separately.
4. Those are often different and the difference matters.
5. Record the governing law and the jurisdiction.
6. Locate the term clause. Record the initial term in months.
7. Record whether renewal is automatic, and the renewal period.
8. Record the notice period required to prevent automatic renewal.
9. A missing notice period is reported as absent, never assumed.
10. Locate the termination clause.
11. Record termination for convenience separately from termination for cause.
12. Record the notice period for each.
13. Record any termination fee.
14. Locate the payment terms. Record the period in days.
15. Record whether the period runs from invoice date or receipt date.
16. Record any late-payment interest rate.
17. Locate the liability cap. Record the amount and what it is a multiple of.
18. Record every carve-out from the cap individually.
19. Carve-outs are where the cap stops meaning what it appears to mean.
20. Locate the indemnities. Record who indemnifies whom, for what.
21. Record any indemnity that is uncapped.
22. Locate the confidentiality clause. Record its survival period.
23. Locate the data-protection clause. Record the processing role of each party.
24. Record any sub-processor consent requirement.
25. Locate the service levels, if any. Record each metric and its target.
26. Record the remedy for each missed service level.
27. A service level with no remedy is a statement of intent. Mark it so.
28. Locate the change-control clause. Record how prices may change.
29. Record any uplift cap and the index it is tied to.
30. Locate the assignment clause. Record whether consent is required.
31. Locate the entire-agreement clause and note anything it excludes.
32. For every value recorded, store the exact sentence it came from.
33. For every value not found, record `not present` and the sections searched.
34. Never record a value inferred from a heading without reading the clause.
35. Flag every clause whose meaning depends on a defined term.
36. Resolve each defined term against the definitions section.
37. A defined term used but never defined is a drafting defect; flag it.
38. Produce the summary table with one row per clause and its source sentence.
39. STOP. Present the summary and state plainly that it is not legal advice.
40. Never characterise a term as favourable, unusual, or acceptable.

## Rules

- Never give legal advice or an opinion on whether a term is reasonable.
- Never infer a value from a heading without reading the clause body.
- Every extracted value carries its source sentence.
- A clause that is absent is recorded as absent, never as a default.
- Flag defined terms that are used but never defined.
- Never summarise away a carve-out from a liability cap.
