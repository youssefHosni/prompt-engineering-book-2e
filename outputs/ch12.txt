--- 12-loops-building.md:139 ----------------------------------

1. Read PROGRESS.md. Note the last commit you processed.
2. List merged pull requests since that commit.
3. If there are none, write "no changes" to PROGRESS.md and stop.
4. Group them into Added, Fixed, Needs attention.
5. Write outputs/digest-<date>.md with all three sections present,
   even if a section is empty.
6. Update PROGRESS.md: last commit processed, date, what you wrote,
   anything you could not classify.
