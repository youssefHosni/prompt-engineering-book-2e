## 1. Delivery windows

Standard delivery is 3 working days from dispatch.

Express delivery is next working day where the collection is booked before the
cut-off in section 2.

Palletised freight over 500 kg is 5 working days regardless of service level.

Working days exclude weekends and public holidays in the country of delivery.

## 2. Booking and cut-off

The cut-off for Express delivery is 14:00 on the day of collection. Bookings
after the cut-off are collected the following working day.

## 3. Fuel surcharge

A fuel surcharge of 4.5% applies to Express deliveries booked after 2026-02-01.

The surcharge is calculated on the carriage element only and is shown as a
separate line on the invoice.

## 4. Packaging

Fragile items require a minimum of 40 mm of void fill on every face.

Shrink-wrapped pallets must not exceed 1.8 m in height, including the pallet
itself.

Items presented in original manufacturer packaging without an outer carton are
carried at the customer's risk.

Liquids must be double-bagged and declared at booking.

## 5. Claims

Claims for loss or damage must be submitted within 14 calendar days of the
delivery date, or of the expected delivery date where the consignment did not
arrive.

Claims require photographs of the packaging as received and the original
consignment note.

Claims below 25 GBP are settled without investigation.

## 6. Liability

Liability is limited to 100 GBP per consignment unless additional cover was
purchased at booking.

Additional cover is available up to 2,500 GBP per consignment.
